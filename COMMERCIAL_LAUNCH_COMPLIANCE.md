# Commercial Launch Compliance Gate

This repository now contains a technical compliance foundation. **It is not a legal opinion and it does not guarantee immunity from claims, complaints, regulatory action or litigation.** Commercial launch requires the operator and each restaurant to complete the business/legal steps below and have the final legal text reviewed by qualified Kenyan counsel/privacy counsel.

## Launch checklist

1. Privacy Notice — published and restaurant-specific.
2. Customer Terms — published and restaurant-specific.
3. Merchant Agreement — signed with each restaurant.
4. Data Processing Agreement — signed where the platform processes restaurant-controlled personal data.
5. Cookie/analytics disclosure — aligned with the actual production scripts and lawful basis.
6. Consent records — versioned, timestamped and linked to the relevant tenant/customer/order.
7. Marketing opt-in/out — separate from order acceptance; never make marketing consent a condition of purchase.
8. Retention policy — configure customer/order/GPS periods and document lawful retention exceptions.
9. Data-rights workflow — access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability requests are recorded and assigned.
10. Data export — manager-side customer export is available for verified requests.
11. Data deletion — requests are reviewed and fulfilled according to the applicable legal basis, retention duties and legal holds.
12. Privacy configuration — each restaurant must set its real privacy/DPO contact and review retention settings.
13. Processor register — verify every production service, purpose, data category, jurisdiction and transfer safeguard.
14. Administrative audit trail — material platform-owner actions remain auditable.
15. Payment/refund terms — publish restaurant-specific terms and keep provider outcomes authoritative.
16. Delivery terms — publish address, fee, tracking, delay and failed-delivery rules.
17. Rider privacy/terms — final rider agreement must match the actual engagement and location-processing model.
18. Responsibility matrix — merchant, platform operator and processors must have documented responsibilities.

## Mandatory pre-launch evidence

- Confirm whether the platform operator and each restaurant must register as a data controller and/or processor with the ODPC.
- Hospitality businesses are among the categories identified by the ODPC as not exempt from mandatory registration based on the small-business turnover/employee exemption.
- Verify the current ODPC registration status/certificate for every entity that requires registration.
- Verify every cross-border processing destination and the applicable safeguard before enabling the relevant provider.
- Complete a privacy/data-flow review and DPIA where the processing presents the applicable level of risk.
- Verify that the published privacy notice matches the real production code, database, providers and retention behaviour.
- Verify that incident escalation can preserve evidence and meet applicable notification deadlines.
- Do not launch using placeholder contact details, placeholder legal entities, or unreviewed template clauses.

## Technical controls now present

- Versioned legal acceptance at checkout.
- Separate optional marketing consent.
- Tenant-scoped consent records.
- Data-subject request intake and manager workflow.
- Customer export endpoint for verified manager handling.
- Tenant privacy configuration.
- Processor register in the Control Centre.
- Privacy incident register in the Control Centre.
- Bounded live-GPS retention cleanup.
- Legal/privacy links across customer and dashboard pages.
- Regression tests for the compliance foundation.

## Important limitation

The system can enforce technical controls, keep evidence and surface compliance work. It cannot determine the correct legal entity, lawful basis, contractual allocation, retention exception, DPA wording, regulator filing, insurance requirement, employment status or liability allocation for a real business without human/legal review.

Before enabling real customer traffic, replace every draft/template notice with the final approved text and complete the ODPC/contractual launch checklist.
