Cookies, Storage & Analytics Notice

Commercial-launch draft. It must be reconciled against the exact production scripts, domains, analytics tags and third-party technologies before publication.

Essential technologies

The service may use browser storage for cart state, order/session state, authentication and security controls. These technologies are used to provide functions the customer has requested and are not presented as optional advertising consent.

Security telemetry

Authenticated dashboards may send limited operational telemetry for security, reliability and troubleshooting. Access is restricted and retention should follow the platform's approved retention schedule.

Optional analytics and advertising

No optional analytics or advertising technology should be enabled in production unless the responsible controller has documented the purpose, lawful basis, recipients, transfer safeguards and any required consent/opt-out mechanism. The processor register must be updated before a new service is enabled.

Third-party content

Payment, mapping, authentication, messaging and hosting services may create their own technical records when their functionality is used. Their current privacy documentation and contractual safeguards should be reviewed as part of the processor register.

Your choices

Where consent is required, it must be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous and there must be a practical withdrawal mechanism. Refusing optional analytics or marketing must not prevent a customer from using functions that do not require those optional purposes.

Changes

The applicable version is identified in the tenant privacy configuration. Material changes should be reflected in the published notice before the relevant technology is enabled.
