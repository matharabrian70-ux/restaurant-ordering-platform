# Data Processing Agreement — Commercial Draft

**Status: counsel-review draft. To be signed where the platform processes restaurant-controlled personal data as a processor.**

## 1. Subject matter and duration
The platform processes personal data for the duration of the restaurant's use of the ordering services and for the limited post-termination period required for lawful retention, dispute preservation, reconciliation or secure deletion.

## 2. Documented purposes
Processing may include customer ordering, delivery operations, customer support, payment coordination, fraud/security controls, communications requested by the restaurant, reporting and agreed platform operations.

The platform must not repurpose restaurant-controlled personal data for unrelated commercial purposes without a lawful basis and the required agreement/notice.

## 3. Categories of data
Depending on enabled features: name, phone, email, delivery address, delivery notes, order history, payment references, customer support records, device/security information, rider/staff account data and operational location information.

## 4. Categories of data subjects
Customers, restaurant staff, managers, riders and other individuals whose information the restaurant lawfully submits through the service.

## 5. Controller instructions
The restaurant/controller provides documented instructions through the signed agreement, configured tenant settings and lawful service requests. The platform must inform the controller if an instruction appears to conflict with applicable data-protection law.

## 6. Confidentiality
Personnel authorised to process the data must be subject to confidentiality obligations appropriate to their role.

## 7. Security measures
The platform maintains proportionate technical and organisational measures including access control, tenant isolation, authentication/session protections, rate limiting, encryption in transit, audit logging, incident monitoring, scoped real-time access, retention controls, security regression testing and bounded recovery.

## 8. Subprocessors
The platform maintains a processor/subprocessor register. Subprocessors may only be enabled after review of purpose, data categories, jurisdiction, transfer safeguards, security and contractual status.

## 9. International transfers
Before enabling a cross-border processor for restaurant-controlled personal data, the parties must document the destination and applicable legal mechanism/safeguards and update the relevant notice/register.

## 10. Data-subject rights
The platform will provide reasonable technical assistance for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability requests. The controller determines whether the request is legally valid and what may be disclosed, subject to the applicable law and the agreed workflow.

## 11. Security incidents
The platform will notify the controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting controller data and will provide available information necessary for the controller's assessment and legally required notification.

The controller remains responsible for determining whether a notification to the Data Commissioner or affected data subjects is required, subject to applicable law and the processor's statutory duties.

## 12. DPIA and risk assistance
Where processing is likely to result in high risk to data subjects, the parties will cooperate reasonably with the controller's Data Protection Impact Assessment and risk-mitigation work. The platform will provide information about relevant technical processing and safeguards.

## 13. Retention
The platform will follow configured retention rules while preserving information where lawful retention, legal holds, payment reconciliation, fraud investigation or dispute resolution requires it.

## 14. Return and deletion
On termination, the platform will return or securely delete controller data according to the signed agreement and lawful retention requirements, except for information that must lawfully be retained.

## 15. Audits and evidence
The platform will maintain evidence of relevant security, consent, access, incident and processing controls and will provide reasonable compliance information to the controller, subject to confidentiality and security restrictions.

## 16. Liability
Liability, indemnity, insurance and audit-cost provisions must be completed in the signed commercial agreement after legal review.

## Schedule A — Processing register
Purpose: ordering, fulfilment, delivery, support, payment coordination and security.

Data: customer identity/contact, delivery details, order information, payment references, rider/staff operational data and configured telemetry.

Retention: tenant-specific schedule, with live GPS subject to a shorter operational limit.

## Schedule B — Approved service providers
The live Control Centre processor register is the source of operational truth. Before launch, each provider must have its current contract/status, jurisdiction and transfer safeguards recorded.

## Schedule C — Security measures
Tenant isolation; RBAC; authentication/session controls; rate limiting; audit logging; encryption in transit; scoped real-time tokens; payment reconciliation; bounded incident recovery; security regression tests.

## Schedule D — Incident cooperation
Platform detects/records/escalates. Controller assesses legal notification. Both parties preserve evidence and cooperate on containment, investigation, remediation and communications.
