# Data Processing Agreement — Launch Draft

**Legal review required.**

This template is for the platform's processing of restaurant-controlled personal data.

## Processing
Purpose: digital ordering, delivery operations, customer support, payment coordination, security and agreed analytics.

Categories: customer identity/contact details, delivery details, order history, payment references, rider/staff account data and operational telemetry as configured.

## Instructions
The platform processes data only for documented merchant instructions and service operation, subject to law and the final signed agreement.

## Confidentiality and security
Access is restricted by tenant, role and session. The platform uses authentication controls, rate limiting, audit trails, encryption in transit, incident monitoring and bounded self-healing.

## Subprocessors
The production processor register must be maintained and attached/referenced in the final DPA. New processors should undergo compliance review before being enabled for personal-data processing.

## Data-subject assistance
The platform provides workflows for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability requests. The merchant remains responsible for verifying requests and making lawful determinations.

## Breach assistance
The platform maintains incident records and escalation workflows. Applicable statutory notification decisions must be made by the responsible controller with legal/privacy oversight.

## International transfers
Any cross-border transfer must be assessed and documented with the applicable safeguards or other lawful basis before launch.

## Deletion/return
On termination, data return and deletion should follow the final merchant agreement and applicable legal retention requirements.
