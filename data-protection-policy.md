# Data Protection Policy — Platform Launch Draft

**Internal governance document — counsel/DPO approval required before commercial launch.**

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes how the platform operator governs personal-data processing and supports restaurant merchants in meeting applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements.

## 2. Governance
For each processing activity, document whether the platform acts as controller, joint controller or processor. The determination must be reflected in the applicable merchant agreement/DPA and processing register.

## 3. Lawful basis
Before processing, identify the appropriate lawful basis. Consent must not be used merely because it is convenient. Where consent is used, it must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous and withdrawable.

## 4. Data minimisation
Collect only information necessary for the stated purpose. Delivery location and live GPS must not be collected or retained beyond the operational purpose.

## 5. Transparency
The applicable Privacy Notice must accurately describe categories, purposes, recipients, transfers, retention, rights, contacts and complaint routes.

## 6. Rights
Requests are logged, verified and assigned. Completion or rejection requires recorded verification evidence and response notes. The responsible controller makes the final legal determination.

## 7. Marketing
Marketing is optional and separately controlled. Opt-in and opt-out evidence must be retained and marketing must not be bundled into necessary order acceptance.

## 8. Retention
Each tenant maintains an approved retention schedule. Automated deletion must not override lawful retention, legal holds, reconciliation, fraud investigation or dispute preservation.

## 9. Security
The platform uses role/tenant isolation, authentication/session controls, rate limiting, encryption in transit, audit trails, incident monitoring, scoped realtime access and regression tests.

## 10. Incidents
Suspected personal-data incidents are recorded immediately, contained, assessed, escalated and preserved for investigation. Regulatory notification decisions are made by the responsible controller with privacy/legal oversight.

## 11. Third parties
Every provider receiving personal data is recorded with purpose, categories, jurisdiction, transfer safeguards and contractual status. Unreviewed providers must not be enabled for production personal-data processing.

## 12. DPIA
A DPIA is required where proposed processing is likely to result in high risk to data subjects or otherwise falls within applicable ODPC requirements/guidance. Location tracking and new high-impact features must be assessed before launch.

## 13. Training and access
Personnel receive role-appropriate privacy/security training. Access is least-privilege and removed when no longer required.

## 14. Records
Maintain evidence of legal versions, consent/acknowledgement, rights requests, processor reviews, incidents, retention settings, security testing and approvals.

## 15. Review
Review this policy at least annually and whenever there is a material processing change, provider change, new tenant model or relevant legal/regulatory change.
