Delivery Terms

Template — restaurant-specific legal review required.

Address and access

Customers are responsible for providing a usable delivery address and reasonable access instructions. The platform may retain delivery information for order fulfilment and support subject to the privacy notice.

Fees

Where advanced delivery is enabled, delivery fees may be calculated from configured route/pricing inputs. The final quoted fee is attached to the order before payment.

Tracking

Live rider location is exposed only to the customer order session authorised for that order and is intended for active delivery operations. Location data is subject to retention limits.

Delays and failed delivery

Traffic, weather, access restrictions, incorrect addresses and other operational conditions may affect delivery times. The restaurant's final delivery policy should specify re-delivery, cancellation and fee treatment.
