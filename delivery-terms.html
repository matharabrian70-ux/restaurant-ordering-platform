DELIVERY

Delivery Terms

Restaurant-specific draft. Final delivery charges, service areas, failed-delivery rules and rider relationship must be confirmed before launch.

1. Delivery address

The customer is responsible for providing a sufficiently accurate address, phone number and access instructions. The restaurant or authorised rider may contact the customer to resolve reasonable access problems.

2. Delivery fee

The applicable delivery fee is shown before payment and recorded with the order. Depending on the restaurant's package, the fee may be based on manual restaurant rules or an automated route calculation.

3. Estimated times

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. Traffic, weather, restaurant preparation time, rider availability, access restrictions and other circumstances can affect delivery.

4. Tracking and live location

Where enabled, the customer may receive live delivery status and limited rider location information for the active order. Live location is not intended to be a general-purpose location service and is subject to access controls and retention limits.

5. Failed delivery

If a rider cannot complete delivery because of an incorrect address, unavailable recipient, unsafe access or another customer-controlled issue, the restaurant's published failed-delivery rules apply. Any re-delivery fee must be disclosed before it is charged.

6. Delivery problems

If an order is late, damaged, incomplete or not delivered, contact the restaurant promptly using the published support contact so the event can be investigated and the applicable remedy assessed.

7. Rider safety

Riders may decline or escalate a delivery where the circumstances create a genuine safety concern. Safety decisions should be handled under the restaurant/platform rider agreement and applicable law.
