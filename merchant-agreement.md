# Restaurant Merchant Agreement — Launch Draft

**Legal review required before signing or commercial launch.**

This template is intended to define responsibilities between the ordering platform operator and each restaurant merchant.

## 1. Roles
- The restaurant is responsible for the purposes and instructions for customer, staff and rider data used for its restaurant operations, subject to the final legal allocation.
- The platform operator provides software, hosting/integration and operational tooling and may process personal data on the restaurant's behalf.
- Each party must only use personal data for authorised purposes.

## 2. Restaurant responsibilities
- Publish accurate privacy, ordering, delivery and refund information.
- Configure a valid privacy contact and retention settings.
- Provide lawful instructions and ensure menu/order/customer data supplied to the platform is accurate.
- Restrict manager/rider access and promptly disable leavers.
- Cooperate with data-subject requests and incidents.

## 3. Platform responsibilities
- Maintain tenant isolation and security controls.
- Keep audit records for material administrative and payment/security events.
- Provide data-rights request, consent and incident workflows.
- Maintain a processor register and review cross-border transfer safeguards.

## 4. Security incidents
The parties must notify one another without undue delay after becoming aware of a suspected personal-data breach and cooperate on containment, investigation, notification and remediation.

## 5. Subprocessors and transfers
The final agreement must identify authorised subprocessors and the applicable safeguards for any transfer outside Kenya.

## 6. Retention and deletion
The parties must document retention periods by data category and securely dispose of data when it is no longer required, subject to legal holds and lawful retention requirements.

## 7. Commercial terms
Fees, settlement, refunds, chargebacks, service levels, liability allocation, indemnities, intellectual-property terms and termination consequences must be completed for the final merchant contract.

## 8. Order of precedence
The signed merchant agreement, DPA and restaurant-specific privacy notice should be read together. Counsel should confirm which document controls if provisions conflict.
