# Restaurant Merchant Agreement — Commercial Draft

**Status: counsel-review draft. This document is intended to be completed and signed separately for each restaurant before production customer traffic. It is not legal advice.**

## 1. Parties and service
This agreement is between the platform operator identified in the signed order form and the restaurant identified in that order form. The platform provides digital ordering and, where purchased, delivery, rider, payment, messaging, analytics and integration functionality.

The signed order form must identify the legal name, registration details, address, commercial contact and package for the restaurant.

## 2. Roles and responsibilities
The parties will document the purpose and means of each processing activity. The restaurant generally determines the purposes of customer processing required to sell and fulfil its restaurant services. The platform may process restaurant-controlled information on the restaurant's documented instructions and may separately process limited information for its own security, administration, fraud prevention, billing and platform operations.

The parties must not rely on a generic controller/processor label where the actual processing activity requires a different allocation.

## 3. Restaurant obligations
The restaurant must:
- provide accurate menu, price, allergen, opening-hours and delivery information;
- publish the current customer Privacy Notice, Terms, Delivery Terms and Refund Policy;
- provide a valid privacy/complaints contact and update it when it changes;
- complete the applicable ODPC registration obligations before processing where registration is mandatory;
- provide lawful instructions and identify the applicable lawful bases;
- ensure authorised managers and riders receive appropriate access;
- promptly disable departed staff and report suspected personal-data incidents;
- cooperate with data-subject requests and regulatory enquiries;
- approve the actual retention periods for its records;
- ensure any customer marketing campaign has the required lawful basis and opt-out controls.

## 4. Platform obligations
The platform will maintain the agreed technical controls, including tenant isolation, authentication/session controls, access restrictions, audit records, security monitoring, rate limiting, data-rights workflows and bounded recovery. The platform will not represent a technical security control as a guarantee against every incident.

## 5. Data protection
The parties will execute the DPA where the platform processes restaurant-controlled personal data as a processor. The DPA will identify processing purposes, data categories, data subjects, instructions, security measures, subprocessors, transfers, assistance, incident handling and deletion/return obligations.

## 6. Subprocessors
The current processor register is maintained by the platform. A new provider that will process restaurant-controlled personal data must be assessed before production enablement. The assessment must cover purpose, data categories, jurisdiction, transfer mechanism/safeguards, contractual status and security.

## 7. Cross-border processing
Where a provider processes personal data outside Kenya, the parties must document the destination and applicable transfer mechanism/safeguards before enabling the provider for the relevant data. The Privacy Notice must accurately describe material transfers.

## 8. Data-subject requests
Requests received through the platform are recorded and routed to the responsible restaurant/controller. The platform assists with access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability workflows. Identity verification and the final legal decision remain with the responsible controller unless the signed DPA allocates them differently.

## 9. Personal-data incidents
Each party must notify the other without undue delay after becoming aware of a suspected personal-data breach affecting the other party's data and must cooperate on containment, evidence preservation, investigation and legally required notifications. The controller remains responsible for the applicable regulatory decision, with processor assistance as required by law and the DPA.

## 10. Retention and deletion
The parties will maintain a written retention schedule by data category. Platform deletion routines must not override legal holds, accounting obligations, fraud investigations, payment reconciliation, dispute preservation or other lawful retention requirements.

## 11. Payments, refunds and chargebacks
The restaurant remains responsible for its customer-facing refund/cancellation rules. Payment provider confirmation is authoritative for payment status. The platform records payment and refund events and provides reconciliation controls. Commercial settlement terms belong in the signed order form.

## 12. Delivery and riders
Where the platform provides delivery functionality, the parties must document who contracts with riders, who is responsible for rider insurance/equipment/safety, who determines delivery instructions and how rider location is processed. Nothing in this agreement should be interpreted as creating an employment or contractor relationship without legal review of the actual arrangement.

## 13. Marketing
Marketing is not bundled into the customer's purchase acceptance. The restaurant is responsible for the lawful basis, message content, sender identification, consent/opt-out mechanism and campaign purpose for its marketing.

## 14. Security and access
Restaurant credentials must not be shared. The restaurant must use the role appropriate to each employee. Platform owners may suspend access for security or abuse and will preserve relevant audit evidence.

## 15. Intellectual property and branding
The restaurant grants the platform the limited rights necessary to display its supplied logo, menu, photographs, descriptions, colours and other branding to operate the service. The restaurant confirms it has the right to provide those materials and is responsible for claims arising from materials it supplied.

## 16. Confidentiality
Each party must protect non-public business and personal information received from the other and use it only for authorised purposes.

## 17. Fees, service levels and termination
Fees, package, settlement, service levels, support scope, suspension rights, termination notice, refunds of platform fees, data return/deletion and post-termination access must be completed in the signed commercial order form.

## 18. Liability and indemnity
Liability caps, exclusions, indemnities, insurance and allocation of third-party claims must be completed by qualified Kenyan counsel based on the actual business model. No placeholder limitation should be treated as final.

## 19. Governing law and disputes
The final agreement must specify governing law and an appropriate dispute-resolution process after legal review.

## 20. Order of precedence
The signed order form, Merchant Agreement, DPA, restaurant-specific Privacy Notice and customer-facing terms should state their order of precedence expressly. Counsel should confirm which document controls if provisions conflict.

**Pre-launch condition:** no restaurant should be activated for real customer traffic until the legal entity details, privacy contact, ODPC status, processor/transfer review and signed commercial documents are complete.
