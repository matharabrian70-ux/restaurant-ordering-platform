The ordering journey

Fewer hand-offs.
A clearer path to order.

A good ordering experience should feel natural to the customer and manageable for the restaurant team. The platform connects the core steps into one flow.

01 / DISCOVER

Explore the menu

Present dishes and products in a digital menu built for your restaurant identity.

02 / CONFIGURE

Make it theirs

Support product-specific options and choices before items enter the cart.

03 / CHECK OUT

Place the order

Review configured items and move through the checkout flow. Payment options depend on the merchant's configured provider.

04 / OPERATE

Keep service moving

Restaurant teams receive and update orders through manager operations; customer tracking follows supported order states.

Built for responsible operation

More than a menu page.

The codebase includes tenant-scoped authorization, controlled package entitlements, audit foundations, payment reconciliation safeguards and a purpose-bound Control Centre approach. Those technical controls support safer operation, but each restaurant deployment still needs configuration, testing and its own legal and privacy review.

Tenant boundariesAccess is resolved against authenticated tenant context and backend package rules.
Payment integrityProvider outcomes and server-side order totals remain authoritative; sandbox and production paths must be tested.
Operational rolesCustomer, manager, rider and platform surfaces follow distinct access and capability rules.
Launch disciplineProduction smoke tests, local merchant terms, data protection and provider setup remain go-live gates.
