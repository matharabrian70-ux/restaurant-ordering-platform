What we collect

Depending on the service used, this may include name, phone number, email, delivery address, delivery notes, order details, payment references, device/browser information used for security, and rider location data while an active delivery is being tracked.

Why we use it

We use information to receive and fulfil orders, calculate delivery, process payments, provide customer support, operate rider delivery, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and—only where separately permitted—send marketing communications.

Lawful basis

The applicable lawful basis depends on the processing activity. Contract performance, legal obligations, legitimate interests and consent may apply. Marketing consent is separate from the consent needed to place an order.

Sharing and transfers

Service providers may process information on behalf of the restaurant or platform. The processor register and current transfer safeguards must be reviewed and kept accurate before launch.

Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose or as required/authorised by law. Restaurant-specific retention settings are stored in the platform and should be reviewed by counsel.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure where applicable, objection, restriction and portability, subject to lawful limitations. Use the data rights request form.

Security and incidents

The platform uses access controls, tenant isolation, rate limiting, audit records and bounded incident monitoring. A security incident does not automatically mean a legally reportable personal-data breach; incidents are assessed through the breach workflow.
