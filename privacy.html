Privacy Notice

Commercial-launch draft reviewed against the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable ODPC regulations/guidance. Final publication requires confirmation of the restaurant's legal identity, contacts, processors, transfers and retention schedule and review by qualified Kenyan privacy counsel.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

The restaurant identified on the ordering experience is responsible for the restaurant-specific processing connected with taking and fulfilling your order. The platform operator may process information on the restaurant's behalf under the merchant agreement and may also be a controller for information it processes for its own security, administration, fraud prevention and platform operations. The exact role for each processing activity is determined by the applicable arrangement and is not changed merely by the label used in this notice.

2. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact information such as name, phone number and email address.
  • Delivery information such as address, landmarks, delivery notes and location coordinates when you choose to provide them.
  • Order information including items, quantities, prices, delivery status and support history.
  • Payment references and status. Full card credentials are handled by the configured payment provider rather than stored as ordinary restaurant order data.
  • Security and technical information such as IP-derived security records, browser/device information and authentication/audit information where necessary.
  • Rider operational information, including account details and live location during an authorised active delivery where the feature is enabled.

3. Why we use information

  • To receive, confirm, prepare and fulfil orders and delivery.
  • To calculate and communicate delivery charges and estimated delivery information.
  • To process and reconcile payments, refunds and payment disputes.
  • To provide customer support and resolve order issues.
  • To protect accounts, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain service security and investigate incidents.
  • To meet legal, accounting, regulatory and dispute-resolution obligations.
  • To send optional marketing communications only where the applicable lawful basis and required consent/opt-out controls have been satisfied.

4. Lawful basis

Different processing activities may rely on different lawful bases, including performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests where appropriate, and consent where consent is the appropriate basis. We do not treat consent as a substitute for another lawful basis where the processing is necessary to perform the service. Optional marketing is kept separate from order acceptance.

5. Location information

Delivery location may be requested to calculate a delivery fee or provide delivery services. Where live rider tracking is enabled, rider location is used for active delivery operations and customer tracking. Live GPS is subject to a configured retention limit. We do not use location information for unrelated purposes merely because it is technically available.

6. Who receives information?

Information may be disclosed to the restaurant, authorised staff and riders where necessary for fulfilment; payment, mapping, messaging, hosting and other service providers that support the platform; professional advisers where necessary; regulators, courts or law-enforcement bodies where legally required; and other recipients where you have been informed and the disclosure is lawful.

7. Transfers outside Kenya

Some service providers may process information outside Kenya. Before a production provider is enabled for personal-data processing, the platform operator and relevant restaurant must identify the destination and document the applicable lawful transfer mechanism and safeguards. The current processor register is maintained in the Control Centre.

8. Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or longer where a lawful retention obligation, dispute, fraud investigation, accounting requirement or other lawful basis applies. Restaurant-specific retention settings are maintained in the platform. Live rider GPS has a shorter operational retention period.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and lawful limitations, you may request information about processing, access your personal data, request correction, object to processing, request erasure where applicable, request restriction, and request portability where applicable. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

10. Marketing

Marketing is optional and separate from ordering. Where consent is used, it is recorded separately and may be withdrawn. Marketing messages must include the applicable opt-out mechanism. Withdrawing marketing consent does not affect processing that is lawful on another basis or the fulfilment of an existing order.

11. Children

The ordering service is not intentionally designed to collect children's personal data for independent accounts. Where processing children's data is required, the responsible controller will apply the additional safeguards required by applicable law and ODPC guidance.

12. Security

Controls include role-based access, tenant isolation, authentication/session protections, rate limiting, audit records, encryption in transit, scoped real-time access, incident monitoring, bounded recovery and security regression testing. No security measure can guarantee that an incident will never occur.

13. Personal-data incidents

Suspected incidents are recorded, contained, assessed and escalated through the platform's privacy incident workflow. Where a statutory notification obligation applies, the responsible controller will make the notification decision and coordinate with the processor and relevant advisers.
