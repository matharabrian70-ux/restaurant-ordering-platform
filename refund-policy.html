REFUNDS

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Restaurant-specific policy template — legal and commercial review required.

Before acceptance

Customer cancellation may be available while an order remains NEW. If a paid order is cancelled and is eligible for a refund, the system creates an idempotent refund intent and records the provider outcome.

After acceptance

Cancellation and refund eligibility can change after restaurant acceptance or delivery progression. The restaurant should publish its specific conditions.

Provider timing

A refund marked processed by the payment provider may still take additional working days to appear in the customer's account.

Disputed transactions

Customers should first contact the restaurant/platform using the published support contact. The platform maintains audit records for payment and refund events.
