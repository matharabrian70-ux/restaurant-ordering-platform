Refund & Cancellation Policy

Restaurant-specific commercial draft. The restaurant must approve the actual rules and qualified Kenyan counsel should review any exclusion, limitation or consumer-rights wording before launch.

1. Before restaurant acceptance

If the restaurant has not accepted the order, cancellation may be available subject to the ordering system's status and the payment state.

2. After acceptance or preparation

Once an order has been accepted or preparation has started, cancellation and refund eligibility may change because food may have been prepared or committed for delivery. The restaurant's configured policy must state the applicable conditions.

3. Incorrect, unavailable or failed orders

If the restaurant cannot provide an accepted item, or a material fulfilment failure occurs, the customer should contact the restaurant promptly. The restaurant will assess the appropriate remedy under its policy and applicable law.

4. Payment and duplicate charges

If a payment appears duplicated or a payment was captured for an order that was not successfully created, contact support with the payment reference. The platform will reconcile the transaction against provider records rather than relying solely on the customer's browser state.

5. Refund processing

Where a refund is approved, the platform records a refund intent and provider outcome. Provider and bank processing times may mean that the refund does not appear immediately.

6. Chargebacks and disputes

Customers should first contact the restaurant/platform to resolve an issue. Nothing in this policy removes a mandatory right to use an available payment-dispute, consumer-protection or regulatory process.

7. Contact

Use the restaurant support contact displayed at checkout and in the Privacy Notice.
