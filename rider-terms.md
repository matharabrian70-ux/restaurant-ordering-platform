# Rider Privacy & Use Terms — Launch Draft

**Final wording must be reviewed before commercial rider onboarding.**

Riders may use the rider dashboard only for authorised delivery work.

Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, order details and live delivery location information are confidential operational information. Riders must not copy, disclose, sell, reuse or publish this information outside the authorised delivery purpose.

The dashboard may use location services while an active delivery is being performed. Location information is limited by the platform's retention and access controls.

Riders are responsible for protecting passwords, devices and session access. Suspected account or privacy incidents must be reported promptly through the restaurant/platform support channel.

The final rider contract should also address the commercial engagement, compensation, safety, insurance, tax, equipment, dispute resolution and termination arrangements applicable to the rider.
