# Rider Terms & Privacy Notice — Commercial Draft

**Status: counsel-review draft. The actual rider engagement model, employment/contractor status, compensation, insurance and safety obligations must be confirmed by Kenyan counsel before onboarding paid riders.**

## 1. Purpose
The rider dashboard is provided only for authorised delivery operations. Access may be restricted or suspended where necessary for security, fraud prevention, misuse or operational safety.

## 2. Personal information used for delivery
Depending on the restaurant's enabled features, the rider may receive customer name, phone number, delivery address, delivery notes, order details and limited information required to complete the delivery.

## 3. Confidentiality
Customer and restaurant information is confidential. Riders must not copy, photograph, disclose, sell, publish or reuse customer information outside the authorised delivery purpose.

## 4. Location
When an active delivery requires live tracking, the rider dashboard may collect and transmit location information to the platform and authorised restaurant/customer views. The feature is limited to the operational purpose and subject to access controls and retention limits.

## 5. Device and account security
Riders must protect passwords, devices and active sessions, must not share credentials, and must promptly report suspected compromise or privacy incidents.

## 6. Customer contact
Riders must use customer contact information only to coordinate the authorised delivery. Personal contact details must not be retained or reused for unrelated purposes.

## 7. Safety
Riders may escalate or decline circumstances that create a genuine safety concern through the restaurant/platform procedure. The final safety, insurance and incident rules must be included in the applicable rider agreement.

## 8. Commercial relationship
Nothing in this privacy/use document determines employment, agency or independent-contractor status. The actual signed rider agreement and applicable Kenyan law determine that relationship.

## 9. Data incidents
A suspected loss, disclosure, unauthorised access or misuse of customer/restaurant data must be reported promptly to the designated support channel.

## 10. Ending access
When rider access ends, credentials and device access must be disabled or revoked and retained customer information must be handled according to the applicable retention/deletion rules.

## 11. Final contract
The signed rider agreement must separately address compensation, payment, insurance, equipment, taxes, safety, dispute resolution, intellectual property and termination.
