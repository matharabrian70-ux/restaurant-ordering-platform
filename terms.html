Ordering

Orders are requests to purchase food and related delivery services from the selected restaurant. The restaurant may accept, reject or cancel an order subject to the published ordering rules.

Prices and payment

Prices shown at checkout are generated from the restaurant's current menu and applicable delivery pricing. The platform uses the payment provider selected by the restaurant. Payment is not considered successful merely because a checkout request was submitted; the platform records payment only after provider confirmation.

Refunds and cancellations

Cancellation and refund eligibility depends on order status, payment status and the restaurant's published policy. Refund timing can depend on the payment provider and financial institution.

Delivery

Delivery addresses and notes must be accurate enough to complete delivery. Delivery estimates are estimates. A rider may use location services while an active delivery is in progress where the feature is enabled.

Acceptable use

Do not abuse ordering, payment, delivery, SMS, location or authentication functions, submit fraudulent information, or attempt to access another customer, restaurant or rider's data.

Disputes

Restaurant-specific commercial terms, applicable Kenyan law and the final merchant/customer agreement govern disputes. This template requires legal review before publication.
